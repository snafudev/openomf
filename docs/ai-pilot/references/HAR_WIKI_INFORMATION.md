**Stats**:
- Forward Speed: 4.3 | Back Speed: 3.6
- Jump Speed: 13 | Fall Speed: 1.05
- Armor: 100 | Stun Res: 27.5

**Tournament Upgrades**:
- Arm Power: 7 | Arm Speed: 7 | Leg Power: 8 | Leg Speed: 6 | Armor: 7 | Stun Res: 8

**Key Moves**:
- Stasis Activator (qcf+P) - Freezes opponent for combo setup
- Matter Phasing (qcb+K) - Teleport kick, escape move

- Heavy combo-hitting glass cannon with surprising power

- Good for unpredictable gameplay against humans

**Stats**:
- Forward Speed: 4 | Back Speed: 3.2

- Rising Talon (qcf+P) - Dragon punch anti-air
- Flying Talon (d,df,f+P) - Rising arc anti-air attack. Launches upward and can be cancelled at apex into Wing Charge or Diving Claw for combo/repositioning. Directional control available: hold Back to change trajectory angle, or Forward to adjust launch direction.

- Air Wing Charge (air f,f+P) - Aerial version
- Diving Claw (air d+K) - Signature move: fast, zero startup, airable

- Emperor of the air: best aerial bot in game
- Deny opponent the air and jumping attacks
- Diving Claw for harassment and punishment
- Rising Talon as anti-air
- Control movement with air presence
- Only weakness: getting cornered

## 6. Jaguar (HAR ID 5)

**Creator**: Ibrahim Hothe  
**Creation Date**: December 4, 2070  
**Special Abilities**: Jaguar Leap, Concussion Cannon, Overhead Throw

**Stats**:
- Forward Speed: 5 | Back Speed: 4
- Jump Speed: 14.5 | Fall Speed: 1.15
- Armor: 100 | Stun Res: 27.5

**Tournament Upgrades**:
- Arm Power: 6 | Arm Speed: 7 | Leg Power: 7 | Leg Speed: 9 | Armor: 6 | Stun Res: 9

**Key Moves**:
- Jaguar Leap (qcf+P) - Forward charge, high priority
- Shadow Jag Leap (hcf+P) - Twice through opponent
- Concussion Cannon (qcb+P) - Controllable projectile
- Overhead Throw **(jump), D+P or (air) D+P** - **Hits in a circle around Jaguar** (not just above). Opponent can be in front, behind, or above as long as in range. Invincible to high moves on startup. Best throw in game. Primary usage: air-to-ground move (jump over opponent and throw down). Can also catch jumping opponents in front. Designed to counter anti-air attacks and punish on landing.
- Suplex (close f+P) - Ground throw

**Strategy**:
- Ultimate beginner bot: easy to learn, hard to master
- Overhead Throw is best move: prevents jumping and handles multiple positioning angles
- Use primarily as air-to-ground setup (jump over opponent, throw as they rise or approach)
- The circular hitbox makes it extremely strong—beats basically anything in that radius
- Heavy on special moves and throw combos
- Excellent air control with Overhead Throw
- Weaker against human players than AI
- Relatively beginner-friendly playstyle
